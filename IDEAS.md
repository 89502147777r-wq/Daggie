## Robot builder + test track (2026-10-17)
Build a robot from many different parts, then send what you built down a test track.
- **Garage:** bodies (crate, barrel, toaster, fridge), running gear (wheels, tracks, legs, skis), arms (claw, hammer, saw), engines (rocket booster, propeller), heads. Parts snap to points on the body with a finger; weight and centre of mass are real.
- **Test track:** run-up, ramp, saw, wall, press, finish. Result: made it, flipped, or fell apart.
- **Physics decides:** a top-heavy robot tips over, a weak joint tears off on impact, too much rocket sends it into the wall.
- **Daggie** rides inside as the pilot / test dummy.
- **Replay** with the cinematic face shots like the crash mix; score: distance, parts that survived.
- **First version:** one body, 3-4 kinds of wheels, a rocket, 2-3 arms, a short track with a ramp and a wall.
